# Articles

### English

- [Introduction to
  mapSpainHist](https://pauvallprat.github.io/mapSpainHist/articles/en/introduction.md):

  First steps: municipal maps of a census, census figures and changes
  over time.

- [Higher-level units and data
  aggregation](https://pauvallprat.github.io/mapSpainHist/articles/en/units.md):

  Historical provinces, judicial districts and electoral districts, and
  how to aggregate municipal data to them.

- [Guide to functions and
  data](https://pauvallprat.github.io/mapSpainHist/articles/en/functions.md):

  Overview of all the functions, arguments and tables of the package.

- [Methodology, sources and
  warnings](https://pauvallprat.github.io/mapSpainHist/articles/en/methodology.md):

  How the maps were built, where the data come from and what their
  limitations are.

- [Problems and
  suggestions](https://pauvallprat.github.io/mapSpainHist/articles/en/contact.md):

  How to report errors or make suggestions about the maps and the data.
