# mapSpainHist: Historical Municipal Boundaries of Spain (1900-2001)

Municipal boundaries for each population census between 1900 and 2001,
plus provinces, judicial districts and electoral districts built from
them. All maps derive from a single partition of the territory, so they
have no gaps or overlaps and aggregate consistently.

## Warning

approximate boundaries: For most historical municipalities there is no
cartography of their limits. Their polygons were reconstructed
approximately from the earliest available official municipal division
(early 2000s), making sure that the municipal seat (the settlement that
usually names the municipality) and, when known, the other settlements
of the municipality fall inside the polygon. The maps are especially
useful to represent municipal data graphically, but they must be used
with caution when geographic variables matter (area, distances, exact
borders): they are a useful but imperfect approximation.

## Licences and sources

The code of the package is released under the MIT licence. The data
(geometries, \[censos\] and \[unitats\]) are released under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). The reference
municipal boundaries derive from SIANE (Instituto Geográfico Nacional,
CC BY 4.0); census figures come from the population censuses of the
Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE); municipal changes from
\*Variaciones de los municipios de España desde 1842\* (MINAP); the 1920
electoral districts from the source cited in the methodology.

The full methodology is available on the package website (in Catalan,
Spanish and English).
